ILI9341_t3 font-format and conversion notice

Historical upstream conversion route
------------------------------------
Tool:
  bdf_to_ili9341.c
  Purpose: convert BDF font data to the packed ILI9341_t3 font-table format
  Copyright: 2015 Paul Stoffregen
  Upstream: https://github.com/PaulStoffregen/ILI9341_t3/tree/master/extras
  License: GNU General Public License version 3 or later

Historical wrapper command:
  otf2bdf -p <size> <font.ttf> | ./bdf_to_ili9341

Pre-converter:
  otf2bdf
  Purpose: convert OpenType/TrueType outline fonts to BDF
  License: MIT

This route is recorded only as historical background. TP-3000 does not
redistribute executable copies of these converters, and current generated font
data does not depend on their output.

Current TP-3000 generators
--------------------------
Files:
  tools/font_provenance/generate_droidsansmono.py
  tools/font_provenance/generate_fontawesome_f080_40.py

License:
  GPL-3.0-only

Purpose:
  Independently rasterize the required glyphs from the verified AOSP Droid Sans
  Mono and official Font Awesome 4.5.0 TTF files and emit
  ILI9341_t3_font_t-compatible packed C representations.

The project-local generators implement the packed output themselves and do not
read or copy historical ILI9341_fonts C tables. Generated Droid Sans Mono data
remains under Apache-2.0; generated Font Awesome data remains under SIL Open
Font License 1.1. The generators' GPL license does not replace those font-data
licenses.

The complete GPL version 3, MIT, Apache-2.0 and OFL-1.1 texts are supplied in
LICENSES/ or the corresponding bundled component folders.
