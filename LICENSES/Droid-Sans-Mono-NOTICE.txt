The generator verifies the source hash and creates all 18 TP-3000 sizes at
100 dpi with monochrome FreeType rasterization. It writes the packed glyph
data, index tables and ILI9341_t3_font_t-compatible descriptors itself. It does
not read or copy the historical ILI9341_fonts C tables.

The small percent sign is derived in each generator run from the freshly
rasterized U+0025 glyph. It is area-resampled to 75%, centered in the same
monospaced cell and aligned to the same baseline. No earlier small-percent
bitmap is copied.
